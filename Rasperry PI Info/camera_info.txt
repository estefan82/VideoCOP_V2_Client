1. Command Line Tools (CLI)
CLI tools are the most powerful for scripting and deep hardware configuration. Most of these require the v4l-utils package.

v4l2-ctl: The Swiss Army knife for Linux cameras.

List devices:

v4l2-ctl --list-devices

List controls:

v4l2-ctl -d /dev/video0 --list-ctrls (shows brightness, focus, exposure, etc.).

Set a control:

v4l2-ctl -d /dev/video0 --set-ctrl=brightness=128
